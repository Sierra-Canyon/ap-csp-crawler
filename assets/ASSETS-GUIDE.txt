HOW TO PUT YOUR OWN ART AND SOUND IN THE GAME
=============================================

Looking for ready-made art and music, or want a chatbot to help you make some?
Open  asset-library/index.html: it hands you files that are already named correctly.

Both graphics engines (dungeon-graphics and dungeon-web) read THIS folder.

THE EASY WAY: just replace a file
----------------------------------
Save your picture over one of these, keeping the same name:

    floor.png     the ground                     (tile .)
    wall.png      walls                          (tile #)
    door.png      the way to the next level      (tile +)
    loot.png      treasure                       (tile $)
    player.png    the hero                       (tile @)
    monster.png   any monster                    (tile M)
    npc.png       someone to talk to             (tile N)
    powerup.png   a powerup                      (tile *)
    boss.png      the boss, drawn a bit bigger   (tile B)
    sword.png     the sword, pointing RIGHT
    arrow.png     what F shoots, pointing RIGHT
    stone.png     what T throws
    bolt.png      every aimed spell, pointing RIGHT

Pictures can be ANY size: 16x16, 32x32, 64x64, a photo. They are drawn one tile big.
Use PNG with a see-through background for the player, monsters, loot and sword.

Sounds (.wav, .ogg or .mp3 all work):

    music     loops while you play
    hit       your sword lands            hurt     a monster hits you
    defeat    a monster dies              bump     you walk into a wall
    loot      you open a chest            potion   you drink a potion
    door      you take the stairs         win / lose
    shoot     an arrow or stone leaves    cast     you cast a spell
    powerup   you pick up a *             talk     someone speaks
    roar      the boss changes phase      quake    the ground shakes

Anything missing is simply skipped (or drawn as a colored block), so you can
delete files you do not want. Nothing here can break the game.

ANIMATION: sprite sheets, no settings needed
---------------------------------------------
  * A picture that is WIDER than it is tall, e.g. 32x16 or 64x16, is read as a row of
    square frames and played as an animation.
  * A picture that is 4 squares TALL, e.g. 32x64, is read as a walking sheet:
        row 1 = facing down     row 2 = facing left
        row 3 = facing right    row 4 = facing up
    and the squares across each row are the walking frames. (Look at player.png.)
  * A square picture is a single still image.

If your sheet does not fit those rules (for example 4 frames x 4 directions is square),
describe it with one line in assets.txt.

SPECIAL NAMES
-------------
    monster_grub.png  monster_bat.png  monster_ogre.png    a picture for one kind of monster
                                                           (the monster's name, in lower case)
    wall2.png  floor3.png  door4.png  music2.ogg ...       used only on that level number
    monster4.png   monster_ogre4.png                       monsters on that level number only
    boss_warden.png   boss4.png   boss_dragon4.png         a picture for one boss (its name, lower case, no spaces)
    npc4.png   powerup4.png                                the N and * on that level number only
    spell_zap.png                                          the picture for ONE spell. Without it, bolt.png is used

Level 4 is YOUR level (MyLevel), so wall4.png, floor4.png, door4.png, loot4.png, monster4.png,
npc4.png, boss4.png and music4 give your level its own look without changing levels 1 to 3.
Look in samples/ for three finished examples: water, forest and fire.

If the special file is not there, the plain one (monster.png, wall.png, music) is used.

DOWNLOADED TILESETS
-------------------
Most free tilesets are one big picture with many small squares. Put the file here and
add lines to assets.txt saying which square is which. The examples are in that file.
Only use art and music you made, or that the creator says is free to use, and credit them.
